**The need**: let people follow a mosque's classes live from their phone, and listen back to them afterwards.

**What the app covers**: live audio streaming, notifications, managing classes, teachers and replays, and an admin interface for the mosque.

**My role**: designing and developing the application, in React Native with Expo.

**Current status**: in development. A TestFlight testing phase is approaching; the app is not yet published.
